Help with PeopleOS.

PeopleOS is not accepting public beta support requests yet.

Before this page is published

Add the monitored support email and the operator or company name. These are required release details and have not been supplied.

Reminders

Turn Today reminders on or off and choose a local reminder time in PeopleOS Settings. If permission was denied, allow PeopleOS notifications in iPhone Settings.

Backups

Create a JSON backup from Settings before moving devices or clearing local data. Restoring always shows a preview and validates the file before replacing the local database.

Privacy

Do not include contact names, notes or other people’s personal information in a support request. Read the full privacy policy.
