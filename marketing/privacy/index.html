Privacy

Your relationships stay yours.

PeopleOS is local-first. This policy explains what the app and this small website do with your data.

Effective 11 August 2026

Data in the app

PeopleOS stores the people, contact details, notes, memories, affiliations, follow-ups and relationship history you add on your device. There is no PeopleOS account, hosted PeopleOS API, advertising profile or contact-data analytics service.

Optional iCloud sync

In the iPhone app, you can choose to copy PeopleOS data to your private Apple iCloud database so it can be restored or used on your Apple devices. Sync is off until you enable it. Apple processes the iCloud copy under your Apple account and Apple’s terms. PeopleOS does not receive your Apple ID credentials.

Local reminders

Reminders are optional and use the normal iOS permission. Scheduling and delivery happen on the device through Apple’s local notification framework. Notification previews do not include contact names, notes, reasons or relationship details. PeopleOS uses no notification backend or remote push service.

Import, backup and deletion

PeopleOS reads a vCard only when you choose a file to import. A JSON backup is created only when you request one. You can turn reminders off in PeopleOS or revoke notification permission in iPhone Settings. Removing local app or browser data deletes the on-device copy; private iCloud data remains subject to your iCloud controls and Apple’s retention processes.

This website

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies, run behavioural tracking, or collect a mailing-list address. Following the beta link will be subject to Apple’s TestFlight or App Store terms once that link is live.

Questions

Use the support page for the current support route. A monitored support address and the operator’s legal identity must be published there before the public beta opens.
